# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

People looking for structured fitness tracking and access to a personal coach.

## Product Purpose

FORMA is a fitness and coaching app for recording training and nutrition, tracking progress, and working with a personal trainer.

## Positioning

The product's distinctive positioning beyond combining fitness tracking and personal coaching has not been confirmed.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The app includes workout creation and tracking, nutrition and calorie logging, progress analytics, progress photos, hydration tracking, GPS run tracking, fitness chat, membership plans, trainer assignment, trainer messaging, and scheduled trainer classes.
- Client and trainer capabilities vary by account role and membership access.
- The public trainer directory should show only approved trainers and factual public profile fields already stored in the app. Do not invent credentials or testimonials.

## Brand Commitments

- The product is named FORMA.
- The public redesign uses the color, type, spacing, shape, and component tokens established for the FORMA redesign.

## Evidence on Hand

- Existing feature descriptions and app workflows are in `client/src/pages/Features.jsx` and the routed pages under `client/src/pages/`.
- No approved public testimonials or outcome metrics have been confirmed. Do not fabricate them.

## Product Principles

- Make training and progress legible and trackable.
- Keep claims grounded in functionality that exists in the app.
- Represent trainer credentials and profiles only with verified, stored information.
