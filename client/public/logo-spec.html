The Tri-Semantics Anatomy
How three ascending bars simultaneously resolve into a tracking chart, the letter "F," and human athletic movement.
Color-coded structural decomposition
3

Top Bar + Upward Stroke (Peak Target / Forward Stride / Top Arm of "F")

Spans 17 units before breaking upward into a dynamic 45° surge (20.5, 1.5). In data: represents breakthrough performance and goal attainment. In kinetics: the chest, lead arm, and forward gaze of an athlete surging through the finish line.

2

Middle Bar (Consistent Progress / Mid-Flight Pelvis / Crossbar of "F")

Spans 11 units. The intermediate progression milestone. In kinetics: the driving knee and core propulsion of a runner mid-stride. In typography: establishes the unmistakable Latin crossbar of the "F".

1

Bottom Bar (Baseline / Ground Push-Off / Grounded Foundation)

Spans 6.7 units. The foundational baseline measurement. In kinetics: the push-off foot and trailing drive launching forward from the ground. Its restrained length prevents confusion with an "E", keeping the optical silhouette locked as "F".

Y

Vertical Left Stem (Data Origin / Spine & Core / Letter Stem)

A crisp 3.0-unit spine uniting the three tiers. Acts as the zero-axis in data charting, the athlete's spine and center of gravity, and the primary vertical stroke of the wordmark's initial.

Intentional Rejection of Category Slop Patterns

This monogram intentionally avoids both ubiquitous pitfalls: 1) The generic gradient-blob "AI startup" loop that signals ephemeral vaporware with zero athletic or mechanical credibility, and 2) The literal dumbbell / barbell glyph found across 98% of template fitness trackers. Instead, FORMA commands the visual presence of elite performance tracking equipment — disciplined, athletic, and typographically permanent.
